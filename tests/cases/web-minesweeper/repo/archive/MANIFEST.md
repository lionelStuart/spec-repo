## Archive Rules

- Keep `repo/specs/` and `repo/tasks/` focused on active or near-active work.
- Do not archive an item immediately just because it is done.
- Keep the most recent 5 inactive tasks and 3 inactive specs in active memory unless the project owner defines a different window.
- Archive older `done`, `canceled`, `superseded`, or `deprecated` items after their result and follow-ups are written back.
- Do not archive an item that is still referenced by an active task.
- Do not delete archived project memory unless the project owner explicitly requests it.
